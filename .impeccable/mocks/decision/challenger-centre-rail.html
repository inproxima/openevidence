Anticoagulation for atrial fibrillation in a 78-year-old with CKD stage 4: which agent and dose?

Confirm creatinine clearance first. Edoxaban 30 mg once daily is a Canadian-labelled option at CrCl 15–29 mL/min; apixaban has the best bleeding profile in advanced CKD, but its Canadian monograph gives no dosing recommendation at CrCl 15–24 mL/min.

At CrCl ≥25, apixaban is 5 mg twice daily, reduced to 2.5 mg only when two of age ≥80, weight ≤60 kg or creatinine ≥133 µmol/L apply.

Pivotal trials largely excluded advanced CKD; pooled evidence is heterogeneous.
