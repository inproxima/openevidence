Anticoagulation for atrial fibrillation in a 78-year-old with CKD stage 4 (eGFR 22): which agent and dose?

Quick · 10 sources · 7 cited
Answered 33 s

Confirm creatinine clearance first: eGFR 22 alone is not enough to apply Canadian dosing. Edoxaban 30 mg once daily is a Canadian-labelled option at CrCl 15–29 mL/min3; apixaban has the best bleeding profile in advanced CKD4, but its Canadian monograph gives no dosing recommendation at CrCl 15–24 mL/min.2

Agent and dose

At CrCl ≥25 mL/min, apixaban is 5 mg twice daily, reduced to 2.5 mg only when two of age ≥80, weight ≤60 kg or creatinine ≥133 µmol/L apply2. At 78, the age criterion is not met.2

Pivotal trials largely excluded advanced CKD; pooled evidence is heterogeneous and calls for dosing trials.46
